# Corrections and right of reply

The aim of this repository is accuracy. If anything here is wrong, out of date or unfair, it will be corrected.

## The Dino Den

The operators of The Dino Den, and any owner or staff member whose conduct is described here, are invited to respond. A response will be published in full in this repository, unedited, alongside the evidence. Open an issue, or ask for your statement to be added.

## Everyone else

- **Corrections:** open an issue with the claim, what is wrong, and a source.
- **New evidence:** open an issue with a link or a dated screenshot. Evidence is added only if it can be checked, and it is labelled by how well it is supported.
- **Please do not submit:** real names, addresses, Steam profiles, alleged alt accounts, or private messages between other people. These will be removed. Messages from public Dino Den Discord channels, and messages staff sent to you while acting as staff, are accepted; players' usernames and avatars are hidden before publication, and staff are named by their staff handles (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#messages-from-the-public-dino-den-discord)).
