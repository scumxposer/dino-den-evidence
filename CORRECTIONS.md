# Corrections and right of reply

The aim of this repository is accuracy. If anything here is wrong, out of date or unfair, it will be corrected.

## The Dino Den

The operators of The Dino Den are invited to respond. A response will be published in full in this repository, unedited, alongside the evidence. Open an issue, or ask for your statement to be added.

## Everyone else

- **Corrections:** open an issue with the claim, what is wrong, and a source.
- **New evidence:** open an issue with a link or a dated screenshot. Evidence is added only if it can be checked, and it is labelled by how well it is supported.
- **Please do not submit:** real names, addresses, Steam profiles, alleged alt accounts, private messages, or other people's Discord messages. These will be removed.
