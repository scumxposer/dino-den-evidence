# The Dino Den: Patreon money, casino games and staff conduct

A sourced, one-stop record of how **The Dino Den** — a large unofficial server network for *The Isle: Evrima*, operated by THE DINO DEN LTD (Company No. 16970676, England) — links paid Patreon memberships to casino-style games on its website, and of how its owners and staff treat players. It also collects evidence, documented or reported, of how moderation powers are used, including abuse of those powers, harassment of players and retaliation for criticism, held to the same standards as everything else here.

Everything in the **Documented** sections comes from the operator's own public website, API, FAQ and Terms of Service, captured on **27 October 2026**, from other public records (Patreon's pages, Companies House, legislation), plus a small number of the author's own observations and screenshots shared with the author (sign-in checks, Patreon page screenshots, Discord screenshots and a patron's screenshot), each labelled where it is used. Anyone can re-check the public sources (see [verify-yourself.md](verify-yourself.md)). Player complaints, including reports about staff conduct, are kept separately, labelled as claims, with their sources and counterpoints.

> This repository is not affiliated with The Dino Den, Afterthought LLC (developer of *The Isle*), or Patreon. It is not legal advice. Corrections and responses from The Dino Den are welcome — see [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md).

## The short version

This repository covers two subjects: **the money** (how Patreon payments can end up wagered in casino-style games) and **how the owners and staff treat players** (see [Staff and owner conduct](#staff-and-owner-conduct) below).

On the money side, Patreon money can reach The Dino Den's casino games by two routes, both on the operator's own site. Each step in the first route is the player's choice: a patron can keep the dinosaurs they buy rather than sell them.

```
Patreon pledge (5 tiers, £12–£55 a month as shown on the site)
  └─► AmberShards (18,000–80,000 every 14 days)
        └─► Dino Shop: dinosaurs for 1,000–7,000 AmberShards each
              └─► Marketplace and Auctions: dinosaurs resold to other players for ClawCoins
                    └─► ClawCoins wagered on roll, crash, blackjack, coinflip, jackpot and crates
                          (jackpot: players' bets go into one pot, one player wins it, the site keeps 2.5%)
                          └─► every 100,000 ClawCoins wagered = 1 entry in a weekly 100,000,000-ClawCoin raffle

Patreon pledge ──(1.5x–4.5x ClawCoin earning multiplier as configured; Apex described as 3.5x)──► ClawCoins   (a second route: ClawCoins directly, no sale needed)
```

Patreon is not the only way in: ClawCoins are earned by playing, and AmberShards also come from free login rewards and Twitch watching (per player, a few hundred AmberShards a week from login rewards, against 18,000–80,000 every two weeks from Patreon; Dino Den's Twitch program reports 32M AmberShards paid out in total; see [evidence/01](evidence/01-patreon-to-casino.md#other-ways-to-get-ambershards-for-balance)). This repository is about the paid route.

On 27 October 2026, **117 of 877 active Marketplace listings** were dinosaurs whose IDs match Dino Shop purchases (interpretation explained in [evidence/01](evidence/01-patreon-to-casino.md)), listed for sale in ClawCoins — 37 of them Tyrannosaurus. How many shop dinosaurs are sold rather than played, and how much of the proceeds is wagered, cannot be measured from public data.

## Staff and owner conduct

What the operator's own rules say:

- The current Terms (§5) let staff suspend or terminate accounts *"with or without reason, and without prior notice or compensation"*, and say *"Moderation decisions are made at our sole discretion and are final."* Enforcement *"may extend across the website, our game servers, and connected community spaces such as our Discord."*
- The FAQ tells banned players to *"Open an appeal ticket on our Discord and we will tell you the exact reason and show the evidence where possible."* Reports and appeals go through Discord tickets only.
- The January 2026 Terms said: *"Where reasonable, we may provide notice or an opportunity to appeal via our support channels."*

What the owner and staff have done in Dino Den's own public Discord channels ([evidence/05](evidence/05-staff-conduct.md)):

- A `#wall-of-shame` channel, started on 15 Aug 2026, where the owner, Vire, and a Head Admin, CHUGGY, post other people's messages, including private messages and support-ticket messages, for the server to react to. After a banned player who had asked for a refund apologised, the owner told them: *"You deserve the #wall-of-shame tbh xD"*.
- In that exchange the owner refused the refund (*"We dont refund for players breaking rules"*) and suggested opening a ticket, then said *"I got no clue what you did to get banned."* The player then said they had posted an accusation against the owner, and later apologised. To another permanently banned player, who had messaged him privately, he replied with laughing emojis and *"Dw i will unban you"*, and later wrote that he had *"unbanned a guy for this"*.
- A `#sent-to-hell` "ban trap" whose bot notice says anyone who posts in it is instantly banned (*"No warnings. No second chances."*); its description reads *"Souls claimed: 403 🔥 Type here and join them 😈"*. [Trap channels](https://discordbotlist.com/bots/honeypot/upvote) are a known Discord tool against spam bots and hacked accounts that post in every channel, but this one's description invites people to post.

Players have reported a ban after criticising the server in a Steam discussion, admins using their powers against players in four YouTube videos, a former trial admin who says he was stripped of his role and muted after criticising the owner in staff chat, and (from one player) cheaters not being dealt with. One player says admins have treated them reasonably. These player reports are **unverified claims**, listed with counterpoints in [community-reports.md](community-reports.md). If you have evidence, [submit it](../../issues/new/choose) following [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#evidence-about-owner-and-staff-conduct).

## Why it matters

Patreon's [Commerce Guidelines](https://patreon.com/policy/benefits) (§5, "Gambling, Raffles, and Sweepstakes") say: *"Fans and creators may not use Patreon to access, provide, or facilitate gambling for money or an exchange of value, including digital currencies."* They list *"raffles, casinos and casino games"* among gambling activities. A report has been filed with Patreon Trust & Safety — see [reports-filed.md](reports-filed.md). For balance: ClawCoins cannot be cashed out, and Dino Den's Terms ban real-money trading. Whether the games count as gambling for "an exchange of value" under Patreon's rules despite that is for Patreon to decide.

The Dino Den's Terms of Service also require users to be 18 or older, but no age check was seen at sign-in ([DISCREPANCIES.md §3](DISCREPANCIES.md#3-age-requirement-changed)).

How staff use their powers matters for the same players: dinosaurs, currency and items bought with Patreon perks all sit on accounts that the Terms let the operator suspend *"with or without reason"*, and the Terms also allow staff to *"remove Virtual Items"*, with decisions described as *"final."*

## What this repository deliberately does not include

- Accusations of crimes (for example, DDoS attacks on rival servers). Rumours of this circulate on forums, but no evidence, target or date has been published. See [community-reports.md](community-reports.md#claims-we-do-not-repeat).
- Real names, Steam profile links, alleged alt accounts, or private messages between other people.
- Players' usernames or avatars. Public posts (Steam, Reddit, public Dino Den Discord channels) are quoted or shown with names and avatars hidden, with the channel or link and the date. People who published their own analyses are credited by the name they published under.
- Anything about owners' or staff members' private lives or real identities. Owners and staff are named by the handles they use as staff, with their role, and only for what they did in that role. Official announcements from the operator's Discord app, and official channels the operator itself lists, can be cited.
- The Dino Den's software or other copyrighted material — only findings about it.
