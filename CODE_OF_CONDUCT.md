# Code of Conduct

This repository documents a dispute that involves real people. It only works if it stays factual and fair.

## Expected

- Stick to what you have seen yourself or can link to.
- Be clear about what is fact, what is your experience, and what is opinion.
- Disagree with evidence, not insults. Counter-evidence is welcome, including evidence that supports The Dino Den.
- Respect corrections, even when they go against your view.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, threats or abuse of anyone, including The Dino Den's owners, staff and players.
- Calls to raid, spam, review-bomb, DDoS or otherwise attack the server, its Discord, its website or anyone's accounts.
- Posting personal information: real names, addresses, Steam profiles, alleged alt accounts, or private messages between other people. Evidence about staff is limited to what they did as staff, and usernames and avatars are hidden in anything published.
- Accusing named people of crimes without published evidence.
- Impersonating the operators, staff or anyone else.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or hide comments, close or lock issues, remove content and block users who break these rules. Personal information is removed as soon as it is seen.

To flag a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue (the "…" menu), or open an issue describing it without repeating any personal information.
