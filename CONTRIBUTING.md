# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this record accurate. The repository covers two things: how The Dino Den's Patreon tiers feed its casino-style games, and how its owners and staff treat players. The most useful contribution is **evidence someone else can check**, and corrections when something here is wrong.

## How to contribute

**[Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose)** using the *Submit evidence* form. Use a plain issue for corrections, and say which file and line is wrong and what the source says instead.

Pull requests are only needed for small fixes such as typos or broken links. New findings should come in as issues so they can be checked first.

## What helps most

- Original, uncropped screenshots that show the date and time.
- Video clips with timestamps.
- Links to public pages, posts or archived copies (for example the [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/save)).
- Anything that can be re-checked: an API endpoint, a page on the site, a public post.

## Evidence about owner and staff conduct

In scope: how the owners and staff use their powers and treat players. For example, bans, mutes, strikes or wipes without a stated reason, retaliation for criticism, harassment or abuse directed at players, removing dinosaurs or items, favouritism, or misuse of admin tools.

- Show the whole exchange, not just the reply. Include what led up to it.
- Include the date, the server or channel, and any ban or mute notice or ticket transcript.
- Messages a staff member or owner sent **to you** while acting as staff (including in tickets or DMs) are accepted. Only the relevant part is published, with ordinary players' names hidden.
- Owners and staff are referred to only by the names they use as staff, and only for what they did in that role. Nothing about their private lives, real identities or personal accounts.
- Reports are labelled as claims unless they can be checked, listed with any counterpoints, and the operator is invited to respond.

## What is not accepted

- Real names, addresses, Steam profiles, alleged alt accounts, or private messages between other people. These will be removed.
- Anything about someone's private life, or drama unrelated to how the server is run.
- Accusations of crimes without published evidence.
- Anything shared to target, harass or pile on the owners, staff or players.

## How submissions are handled

1. Every submission is checked against its source before anything is added.
2. What is added is labelled by how well it is supported: **Documented** (from the operator's own pages, data, terms or official announcements, or evidence anyone can check directly), **Supported** (independent technical evidence, not conclusive), **Player claims** (unverified, listed with counterpoints), **Unconfirmed**, or **Not supported** (checked and not borne out).
3. Conflicts between sources go in [DISCREPANCIES.md](DISCREPANCIES.md), with both sources and what can and cannot be concluded.
4. Every change is logged in [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md), including mistakes in earlier versions.
5. Every change follows the same procedure, written down in [.github/MAINTAINING.md](.github/MAINTAINING.md).

Submissions that can't be checked are not added, but they may be kept open in case more evidence turns up.

## Responses from The Dino Den

The operators, and any owner or staff member whose conduct is described here, are welcome to respond. A response will be published in full, unedited, alongside the evidence. See [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md).
