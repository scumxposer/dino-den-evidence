## Who runs it

The site footer and both versions of the Terms name **THE DINO DEN LTD (Company No. 16970676), registered in England**, with a registered office at 3rd Floor, 86-90 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NE. Companies House lists it as an active private limited company incorporated on 19 January 2026 — <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/16970676>. ## The operator's position, and the evidence

| What the operator says | What the site does |
|---|---|
| Terms §2: games *"are played exclusively with virtual currency, are provided for entertainment only and are not gambling for money or anything of monetary value."* | The currency is supplied by paid Patreon tiers, directly as AmberShards and through the ClawCoin multiplier. AmberShards convert to ClawCoins through the operator's own shop and Marketplace ([01](01-patreon-to-casino.md)). |
| Terms §3: Patreon *"memberships are not purchases of Virtual Items."* | Each tier is defined by the AmberShards it pays; the FAQ says upgrades *"grant the difference between the tiers immediately."* |
| Terms §2: Virtual Items *"have no monetary or real-world value."* | Dinosaurs bought with Patreon-funded AmberShards are listed for ClawCoins, which are wagered for more ClawCoins, dinosaurs and skins. |
| Earlier Terms §7, *"Chance-Based Games (Entertainment Only)"*: *"Any chance-based features (including, without limitation, coin flips, jackpots, loot boxes, plinko, spins, raffles, or similar mechanics) are provided solely for entertainment and use only virtual in-service currency or virtual items."* ([archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20260402000811/https://na.dinoden.gg/terms)) | The operator's own earlier Terms describe these features as chance-based games, including raffles and loot boxes. |
| FAQ: *"ClawCoins are earned by playing - the only real-money support is our Patreon."* | Consistent with the evidence: Patreon is the real-money entry point. |
| Current Terms §1: *"You must be at least 18 years old to use the service."* | The earlier Terms §4 said: *"If you are a minor, you may use the Service only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian (where required by law)."* ([archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20260402000811/https://na.dinoden.gg/terms)). The author observed no age check at sign-in in October 2026 (author's own observation; not re-tested). |

All conflicts between sources are also listed side by side in [DISCREPANCIES.md](../DISCREPANCIES.md).

## Real-money trading

The Terms and FAQ ban players from buying or selling ClawCoins, dinosaurs or accounts for real money. That rule applies between players; it does not cover the operator's own sale of currency-paying memberships through Patreon.

## Privacy policy notes

The privacy policy says the service stores players' in-game position, vitals, playtime, spawns, deaths and kills linked to their SteamID, plus IP address and browser information at sign-in. It does not mention data sent by the DinoDen Client (see [04](04-client-analysis.md)).
