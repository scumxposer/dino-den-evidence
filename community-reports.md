# Community reports

These are **claims made by players** in public forums. Unless marked otherwise, none of them has been independently verified. They are included because several different people describe the same problems over many months, and each is listed with its source and with the counterpoints raised at the time. Dates without a year in Steam threads are 2026.

Reports about how the owners and staff treat players are collected here too (see *Bans after criticism*, *Videos alleging admin abuse* and *Cheaters not dealt with; admins absent* below). What the operator's own rules say about moderation is summarised in the README under [Staff and owner conduct](README.md#staff-and-owner-conduct). New evidence is welcome through the [evidence form](../../issues/new/choose).

Most of the public discussion is on Steam's *The Isle* General Discussions forum. Reddit could not be accessed directly for this repository; the Reddit posts cited below are from screenshots held by the author. One of the Steam threads cited ("Do not play on Petite Pieds…") is mainly about a different server, Petits Pieds; only the parts about Dino Den are used here.

## Recurring complaints

### Patreon perks advertised but not delivered
- The author of a Steam thread, who says he was a trial admin at Dino Den, wrote that advertised perks did not match what patrons got: lower tiers were advertised with *"Apex=Rex"*; patrons were promised *"Priority Queue"* and then told *"You get it when it's active"*; and the top tier was advertised to *"get past the cap (Pop Control)"* when, he says, there was no dino cap at the time. — [Steam thread, 9 Nov 2025, edited Jan 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/669474588663616129/)
- **Counterpoints:** he had his own dispute with Dino Den's staff (see *Bans after criticism*). Some of the perks he mentions are now documented as offered (see the next section).

### Restricted dinosaurs unlocked by paying
- *"But dinoden restrict the Rexs and big carnivore."* … *"BUT, if your apex (paying monthly) your able to play watever you want."* (31 Mar 2026). — [Beware of pay to win servers](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/802343728582771226/)
- **Now documented** ([DISCREPANCIES.md §11](DISCREPANCIES.md#11-patreon-perks-discord-announcement-vs-the-sites-patreon-page)): the operator's Discord announcement of 8 Dec 2025 advertised *"Unlock currently locked dinos"* and *"Apex Role access to Rex – Apex tier supporters can play Rex even when it's locked"*. Rex became officially playable in *The Isle* on 28 Dec 2025, but Dino Den caps how many of each species can be on a server, and "locked" refers to that cap. The perk was still offered in October 2026: the site's patron-only page lets patrons unlock species on a server, a patron's October 2026 screenshot shows Tyrannosaurus marked "APEX+", Patreon's Apex tier listed a bypass of dino cap locks in October 2026, and a Senior Admin (MissElephantuu) wrote on 9 Oct 2026 that *"Allos and rexes can be unlock if you are APEX patreon, otherwise its popcontrol on them."*
- **Counterpoints:** two players replying on Reddit (27 Oct 2026, screenshots held by the author) say a patron's unlock opens the species *"for EVERYONE. Not just themselves"*, and that *"asking in chat will normally get them unlocked, though access to trex is a bit more difficult."* The site's code is consistent with this: it treats an unlock as a server-wide state for a limited time. One of them also says *"Allo is not hard to get at all"*, agrees that Apex patrons *"have more access to trex"*, and says population control on Rex *"is a smart decision."* Another player says they have 30 million ClawCoins without ever subscribing and *"access to any Dino that the paying players have."* ClawCoins can buy dinosaurs from other players on the Marketplace, and a third player says selling shop dinosaurs for ClawCoins *"benefits EVERYBODY involved"*, because players who don't pay can then buy full-grown dinosaurs with ClawCoins.
- Related documented fact: 37 shop-bought Tyrannosaurus were listed on the Marketplace on 27 Oct 2026 ([evidence/01](evidence/01-patreon-to-casino.md)).

### Cheaters not dealt with; admins absent
- Players (Jul–Aug 2026) say banned cheaters return quickly (*"it only last for a day or two then they're back cheating"*), admins are offline in the daytime (*"they all are Offline in the morning / daytime"*), and admins mostly moderate chat (*"they are only 'Chat admins'"*). — [Dino Den faking numbers and botting](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/573793348713953746/), [Honest Opinion thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/582804934666261638/), [Hackers on AU1 and AU2](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/581678695778932381/) (that last thread is mainly about hackers on official servers; the "Chat admins" comment is in it)
- **Counterpoints:** the author of the Honest Opinion thread says *"I got probably around 300hrs on the server and 60% of the time it's alright"*, and a frequent critic says he enjoys the server *"at times"*. A player on Reddit (Oct 2026, screenshot held by the author) says *"Admins have always been reasonable in my interactions."* Cheating is also reported on official servers, not only on Dino Den.

### Bans after criticism
- **Related documented evidence:** the owner's public `#wall-of-shame` channel, a refund refused while the owner said he had *"no clue"* why the player was banned, a permanent ban the owner promised by private message to lift, and later said he had lifted, and a `#sent-to-hell` ban trap ([evidence/05](evidence/05-staff-conduct.md)).
- A player says Dino Den admins banned him after he criticised the server in a Steam discussion: *"Some of the Admins saw my post here and decided to Ban me from their server"* (8 Aug 2026). — [Honest Opinion thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/582804934666261638/)
- The former trial admin says he was stripped of his admin role, muted and kicked after criticising the owner in the staff chat (Nov 2025). — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/669474588663616129/)
- **Counterpoints:** no response from Dino Den appears in either thread. In the trial admin's thread, other players say he was banned from a *different* server (Petits Pieds) for livestreaming rule-breaking (*"you got banned because you were constantly livestreaming yourself breaking the rules (no overpacking)"*), and one says *"I was among the people that reported your constant overpacking."* He disputes that, saying he has messages from that server's owner giving another reason. That dispute is about another server, but readers may weigh it when judging his account.

- **Limits:** three are recordings of the game's replay tool, which shows where players were and their names but nothing that marks an account as staff or shows an admin command being used. ## Claims we do not repeat

Forum posts and screenshots circulating about Dino Den also include accusations of crimes (such as DDoS attacks on rival servers), allegations about individuals' private conduct, and alleged leaked messages. These are not reproduced here because **they cannot be checked**. For the accusations of crimes, no evidence has been published: no logs, no named target, no dates, no statement from a hosting provider or an affected server. The leaked messages that circulate have no date, context or known source, and some come from private staff channels.

If you have verifiable evidence, [submit it here](../../issues/new/choose) (see also [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md)). Evidence of a crime should go to the platform concerned or law enforcement, not to a public repository.
