# Community reports

These are **claims made by players** in public forums. Unless marked otherwise, none of them has been independently verified. They are included because several different people describe the same problems over many months, and each is listed with its source and with the counterpoints raised at the time. Dates without a year in Steam threads are 2026. Steam dates are given as Steam shows them in US Pacific time, so a late-evening post may carry the next day's date in UTC.

Reports about how the owners and staff treat players are collected here too (see *Bans after criticism*, *Strike after confronting a chat spammer*, *Videos alleging admin abuse* and *Cheaters not dealt with; admins absent* below). What the operator's own rules say about moderation is summarised in the README under [Staff and owner conduct](README.md#staff-and-owner-conduct). New evidence is welcome through the [evidence form](../../issues/new/choose).

Most of the public discussion is on Steam's *The Isle* General Discussions forum. Several of the Steam quotes come from the same two people: the former trial admin (who also started the "Dino Den faking numbers" thread), and the author of the "Honest Opinion" thread. Each section says where that is the case, so that one person is not counted as several. Reddit could not be accessed directly for this repository; the Reddit posts cited below are from screenshots held by the author. One of the Steam threads cited ("Do not play on Petite Pieds…") is mainly about a different server, Petits Pieds; only the parts about Dino Den are used here.

## Recurring complaints

### Patreon perks advertised but not delivered
- The author of a Steam thread, who says he was a trial admin at Dino Den, wrote that advertised perks did not match what patrons got: lower tiers were advertised with *"Apex=Rex"*; patrons were promised *"Priority Queue"* and then told *"You get it when it's active"*; and the top tier was advertised to *"get past the cap (Pop Control)"* when, he says, there was no dino cap at the time: *"The entire system currently doesn't work"*. — [Steam thread started 9 Nov 2025; the Dino Den part was added after 13 Nov 2025, last edit 5 Jan 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/669474588663616129/)
- **Counterpoints:** he had his own dispute with Dino Den's staff (see *Bans after criticism*). Some of the perks he mentions are now documented as offered (see the next section).

### Restricted dinosaurs unlocked by paying
- *"But dinoden restrict the Rexs and big carnivore."* … *"BUT, if your apex (paying monthly) your able to play watever you want."* (31 Mar 2026). — [Beware of pay to win servers](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/802343728582771226/)
- **Now documented** ([DISCREPANCIES.md §11](DISCREPANCIES.md#11-patreon-perks-discord-announcement-vs-the-sites-patreon-page)): the operator's Discord announcement of 8 Dec 2025 advertised *"Unlock currently locked dinos"* and *"Apex Role access to Rex – Apex tier supporters can play Rex even when it's locked"*. Rex became officially playable in *The Isle* on 28 Dec 2025, but Dino Den caps how many of each species can be on a server, and "locked" refers to that cap. The perk was still offered in October 2026: the site's patron-only page lets patrons unlock species on a server, a patron's screenshot taken between 28 Sep and 27 Oct 2026 shows Tyrannosaurus marked "APEX+", Patreon's Apex tier card lists *"Bypass All Dino Cap Locks"* (the author's screenshot), and a Senior Admin (MissElephantuu) wrote on 9 Oct 2026 that *"Allos and rexes can be unlock if you are APEX patreon, otherwise its popcontrol on them"*.
- **Counterpoints:** two players replying on Reddit (27 Oct 2026, screenshots held by the author). One says a patron's unlock opens the species *"for EVERYONE. Not just themselves"*, and that selling shop dinosaurs for ClawCoins *"benefits EVERYBODY involved"*, because players who don't pay can then buy full-grown dinosaurs with ClawCoins. The site's code is consistent with the first point: it treats an unlock as a server-wide state for a limited time. The other says *"asking in chat will normally get them unlocked, though access to trex is a bit more difficult"* and *"Allo is not hard to get at all"*, agrees that Apex patrons *"have more access to trex"*, and says population control on Rex *"is a smart decision"*. The same player says they have 30 million ClawCoins without ever subscribing and *"access to any Dino that the paying players have."* ClawCoins can buy dinosaurs from other players on the Marketplace.
- A Steam poster (21 Aug 2026) says Dino Den had Rex and Allosaurus *"BEHIND PAYWALLS before the release of the Dinosaurs, putting them behind the $99.00 Patreon"*, and the former trial admin, posting in the same thread, adds that admins also had to pay to play them. — [Unofficials are abusing the system for your money](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/582805931178577143/)
- **Counterpoints:** no $99 tier was found in October 2026, when Patreon's highest full price for US viewers was $79 a month ([DISCREPANCIES.md §2](DISCREPANCIES.md#2-patreon-prices-vs-the-sites-tier-minimums)); no archived copy of the Patreon page from around December 2025 was found, so earlier prices could not be checked. A player in the same thread says that in 120 hours on these servers he *"never had to pay for anything"* and never saw *"dinos that are not released yet"*. The December 2025 announcement did offer Apex supporters Rex *"even when it's locked"*, three weeks before Rex's official release.
- Related documented fact: 37 shop-bought Tyrannosaurus were listed on the Marketplace on 27 Oct 2026 ([evidence/01](evidence/01-patreon-to-casino.md)).

### Cheaters not dealt with; admins absent
- One player, the author of the Honest Opinion thread, posting in three threads (Jul–Aug 2026), says banned cheaters return quickly (*"it only last for a day or two then they're back cheating"*), admins are offline in the daytime (*"they all are Offline in the morning / daytime"*), and admins mostly moderate chat (*"they are only 'Chat admins'"*). — [Dino Den faking numbers and botting](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/573793348713953746/), [Honest Opinion thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/582804934666261638/), [Hackers on AU1 and AU2](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/581678695778932381/) (that last thread is mainly about hackers on official servers; the "Chat admins" comment is in it)
- **Counterpoints:** the same player says *"I got probably around 300hrs on the server and 60% of the time it's alright"* and that he enjoys the server *"at times"*. The same Reddit player who has 30 million ClawCoins (see above; 27 Oct 2026, screenshot held by the author) says *"Admins have always been reasonable in my interactions"*. Cheating is also reported on official servers, not only on Dino Den.

### Bans after criticism
- **Related documented evidence:** the owner's public `#wall-of-shame` channel, a refund refused while the owner said he had *"no clue"* why the player was banned, a permanent ban the owner promised by private message to lift (he later wrote that he had *"unbanned a guy for this"*, most likely the same player), and a `#sent-to-hell` ban trap ([evidence/05](evidence/05-staff-conduct.md)).
- The same player (the author of the Honest Opinion thread) says Dino Den admins banned him after he criticised the server in a Steam discussion: *"Some of the Admins saw my post here and decided to Ban me from their server"* (8 Aug 2026). — [Honest Opinion thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/582804934666261638/)
- The former trial admin says he was stripped of his admin role, muted and kicked after criticising the owner in the staff chat (added to the same post after 13 Nov 2025, when he said he was a trial admin; the post was last edited on 5 Jan 2026). — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/669474588663616129/)
- **Counterpoints:** no response from Dino Den appears in either thread. In the trial admin's thread, another player says he was banned from a *different* server (Petits Pieds) for livestreaming rule-breaking (*"you got banned because you were constantly livestreaming yourself breaking the rules (no overpacking)"*), and the same player says *"I was among the people that reported your constant overpacking"*. He disputes that, saying he has messages from that server's owner giving another reason. That dispute is about another server, but readers may weigh it when judging his account.

### Strike after confronting a chat spammer
- A Steam poster (19 Jun 2026), not one of the two frequent posters above, says that a spammer flooded Dino Den's global chat while moderators did nothing; he *"started calling the guy out in chat, trying to get him to stop"*, was *"immediately disconnected from the client"* and given a strike for *"Chat Toxicity"*, and the spammer carried on. The same poster says that on Discord an admin *"literally replied with a Spongebob meme"* and then wrote *"We do not disclose actions taken to other players, feel free to open an appeal."* He says he has *"the full, unedited 3GB video recording of this entire session"*; a web search on 28 Oct 2026 found no published copy. — [Steam thread, 19 Jun 2026](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/discussions/14/573793023877691970/)
- **Counterpoints:** the thread's title speaks of a ban, but the post describes a disconnection and a strike. The post does not quote what he wrote to the spammer, so whether the strike fitted the rules cannot be judged. The admin's reply points to the appeal process the FAQ describes, and not telling one player what action was taken against another is common moderation practice; the post does not say whether he appealed. The admin's role has not been confirmed, so the handle is not given. No response from Dino Den appears in the thread. Neither of its two replies adds first-hand evidence: one describes a different server, and the other agrees with him but offers only an unsupported accusation, which is not repeated here.

## Claims we do not repeat

Forum posts and screenshots circulating about Dino Den also include accusations of crimes (such as DDoS attacks on rival servers), allegations about individuals' private conduct, and alleged leaked messages. These are not reproduced here because **they cannot be checked**. For the accusations of crimes, no published evidence has been found: no logs, no named target, no dates, no statement from a hosting provider or an affected server. The leaked messages that circulate have no date, context or known source, and some come from private staff channels.

If you have verifiable evidence, [submit it here](../../issues/new/choose) (see also [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md)). Evidence of a crime should go to the platform concerned or law enforcement, not to a public repository.
